Attiéké

A flavorful West African rice dish cooked in a rich tomato and pepper sauce.


Prep time: About 15 minutes. Cook time: 20–30 minutes, depending on the fish and attiéké. Serving: 3-5 people.
Ingredient Steps
  • 4 cups prepared attiékée
  • 1 whole fish or 4 fish portions
  • 1 whole fish or 4 fish portions
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Chili pepper, optional
  • Water, as directed on the attiéké package
  1. Prepare the attiéké according to the package instructions, usually by steaming it..
  2. Clean the fish and season it with salt, pepper, lemon juice, and your preferred spices.
  3. Grill the fish until it is fully cooked..
  4. Chop the tomatoes and onion into small pieces.
  5. Mix the chopped vegetables with a little lemon juice, oil, and optional chili pepper.
  6. Serve the warm attiéké with grilled fish and the fresh tomato-onion salad.
