Jollof Rice

A flavorful West African rice dish cooked in a rich tomato and pepper sauce.


Prep time: 20 Min Cook time: 50 Min Serving: 4-5
Ingredient Steps
  • 3 cups long-grain rice
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 onion
  • 2–3 tbsp tomato paste
  • Curry powder, thyme, salt, and seasoning
  • Chicken or vegetable broth
  1. Rinse the rice thoroughly.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, peppers, and onion.
  3. Cook the blended sauce with oil and tomato paste.
  4. Add seasonings and broth.
  5. Add the rice and mix well.
  6. Cover and cook on low heat until the rice is tender.
