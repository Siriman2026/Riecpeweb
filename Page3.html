FuFu

A flavorful West African rice dish cooked in a rich tomato and pepper sauce.


Prep time: 5 minutes Cook time: 10–15 minutes Serving: 2-4
Ingredient Steps
  • 2 cups fufu floure
  • Water, as directed on the package
  • Extra water if needed
  1. Measure the fufu flour.
  2. Mix the flour with water as instructed for your specific product.
  3. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring continuously.
  4. Keep stirring until the mixture becomes thick and smooth.
  5. Cover briefly if needed, following the package instructions, until fully cooked.
  6. Shape into portions and serve warm with soup or stew.
